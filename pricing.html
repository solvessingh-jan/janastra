Frequently Asked Questions

Can Janastra be white-labeled for our city's brand? +
Yes! Our City Pro and Enterprise plans include full custom branding with your logo, colors, and even a custom domain (e.g., reports.yourcity.gov.in).
Is Janastra listed on the GeM portal? +
We are in the process of registering on GeM. For government and municipal clients, we can supply all necessary documentation for procurement and tendering. Contact us to discuss.
What does the one-time license include? +
The perpetual license includes complete source code, white-label rights, 1 year of free updates, and priority support. You can deploy it on your own servers with no ongoing fees.
Can we add custom modules for our specific needs? +
Absolutely. Enterprise clients can request custom modules. We've built features like air quality monitoring, event reporting, and local vendor directories in the past.
Is there a free trial available? +
Yes — we offer a 14-day free trial for the Starter and City Pro plans. No credit card required. Contact us to get started.
